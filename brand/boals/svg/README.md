# boals SVG assets

Traced from the approved [direction 2 brand board](../brandkit-overview-v2.png). All files are true SVG vectors with transparent canvases, editable paths, outlined lettering, and no external fonts or embedded bitmap images. Texture and lighting from the raster presentation are omitted.

| Asset | Variants | Canvas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combined logo | `boals-logo-ink.svg`, `boals-logo-milk.svg`, `boals-logo-butter.svg` | 432 × 128 |
| Logo mark | `boals-mark-ink.svg`, `boals-mark-milk.svg`, `boals-mark-butter.svg` | 128 × 128 |
| Wordmark | `boals-wordmark-ink.svg`, `boals-wordmark-milk.svg` | 288 × 97 |
| Expressions | `boals-face-hi.svg`, `boals-face-thinking.svg`, `boals-face-done.svg` | 128 × 128 |

Use ink on light backgrounds, milk on dark backgrounds, or butter for the companion accent. The butter combined logo has ink lettering. Eyes use a contrasting solid fill; lettering counters are transparent. The three expressions share the same body silhouette.

Palette: Ink `#171717`, Milk `#FAF7EF`, Butter `#F3D75B`.

Open [preview.html](preview.html) to inspect every asset. Import any SVG directly into Figma, Illustrator, or another vector editor. Preserve the viewBox and aspect ratio when resizing; change individual path fills to recolor.

The wordmark is traced artwork rather than editable font text. This preserves the approved letter shapes without requiring a particular typeface.
